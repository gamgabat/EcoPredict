# EcoPredict: Unknown Species Impact Simulation Platform

## Core Problem
Traditional Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) companies like AECOM and WSP Global operate with a massive blind spot: they can only assess known species impacts. Our platform fills this gap by utilizing AI-driven simulation to predict potential *unknown* species and their cascading ecological effects. This is critical for preventing unexpected environmental disruptions, which NGOs often use to combat or litigate development projects later on in their life cycle.

## Core Concept
An AI-powered simulation platform serving as both an alternative to, and a complement for, traditional EIA processes. 

## Target Market & Dual-Pricing Model
1. **Primary Segment (Development Companies)**: Energy, infrastructure, and marine development firms requiring comprehensive EIA reports. Priced at **$3K-$10K per project** setup (averaging 3-7 projects/year). Serves as an affordable EIA alternative emphasizing gap coverage.
2. **Secondary Segment (Environmental NGOs)**: Organizations conducting advocacy campaigns. Priced at **$50-$200/month** for ongoing simulation access and predictive risk tracking.

## Key Features
- **Species Interaction Simulator (Coral Reef Proof-of-Concept)**: Models *Diadema antillarum* (sea urchin), *Porites astreoides*, *Montastraea cavernosa*, and *Siderastrea siderea*. Visualizes cascading effects like herbivore population crashes leading to algal blooms and reef collapse.
- **Interactive Mind Map Interface**: Drag-and-drop relationship mapping with real-time variables adjusting "bottom-up" and "top-down" ecological stressors.
- **Environmental Parameter Control**: Dynamic manipulation of disease progression (e.g. SCLTD), biological stress, temperature, and nitrogen/salinity loading.
- **AI Prediction Engine**: Risk assessment algorithms generating AI-driven gap analysis of unknown species interactions based on existing regional biodiversity datasets.
- **EIA Report Export Engine**: Extracts comprehensive, ES/EIS compliant impact reports for planning permits.

## Critical Success Metrics
- Prediction accuracy for interaction cascades.
- Target of 50% faster preparation than traditional EIA companies.
- Zero unexpected environmental disruptions post-implementation for clients.
- Strong user adoption across both development and NGO sectors.
