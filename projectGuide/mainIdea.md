EcoPredict: Unknown Species Impact Simulation Platform
Core Concept:
AI-powered simulation platform that predicts and visualizes the ecological impact of undiscovered species (80% of Earth's biodiversity) on ecosystem dynamics
Key Features:

Prediction Engine: AI-driven forecasting of unknown species characteristics and ecological roles using existing biodiversity datasets
Interactive Ecosystem Simulation: Real-time visualization of ecosystem changes and species interaction modeling
Dynamic Mind Map Interface: Visual species relationship mapping with interactive attribute adjustment capabilities
Environmental Parameter Control: Real-time manipulation of temperature, salinity, pH, humidity, and other environmental variables
Impact Analysis Reporting: Automated environmental impact assessment and report generation for NGO stakeholders

Target Users:
Environmental NGOs, ecological research institutions, environmental consulting firms
Core Value Proposition:
Enables proactive prediction of potential ecological impacts from undiscovered biodiversity to support evidence-based environmental protection policies and ecosystem conservation strategies
Use Case Scenario:
When assessing environmental impact of new development projects, the platform uses known species data from target regions to simulate potential undiscovered species presence and their cascading effects on ecosystem stability, providing NGOs with predictive insights for environmental advocacy
Technical Foundation:
Machine learning models trained on comprehensive biodiversity databases, ecosystem interaction algorithms, and environmental parameter modeling for species-environment relationship prediction
